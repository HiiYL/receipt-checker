發票對獎 Receipt Checker

Scan a Taiwan e-invoice or type a paper invoice, and it is checked against the Ministry of Finance winning numbers.

Point at the left QR code on the e-invoice (電子發票證明聯). The barcode works too.

Take or pick a photo of the receipt with both QR codes visible.

Saved receipts

    Saved on this device only. Receipts from a period not drawn yet are re-checked automatically after the draw.
